Who We Are

Craftsmanship You Can Trust

Established in 2020, Total Home Construction LLC is a licensed residential contractor specializing in complete home transformations and new construction. We craft tailored solutions for the unique New Mexico climate, ensuring lasting beauty and functionality.

Our comprehensive services include expert remodeling, masonry, concrete work, and energy-efficient window and door replacements. We also offer professional landscaping, painting, drywall, and a range of roofing options. Our experienced team is dedicated to bringing your vision to life, building with precision and quality you can trust.
